# A tenant reads only fully evidenced capacity conclusions

The platform example publishes two written decisions in the tenant's snapshot. Each notice gives the issuer (platform), origin (decision), period, available cores and total observed use. The actual tenant summary joins its own reading with the two public amounts. A dedicated complete platform publication proves those same amounts against the pool and all participating readings, including the other tenant's. The tenant receives the declaration as a sourced base row and never receives the private calculation.

The acceptance example uses two periods. Eleven cores with observations of three and five give three available and eight used. Seventeen cores with four and six give seven available and ten used. The tenant can read the numerator and denominator of its own share in both cases, three of eight and four of ten. The typed input records are `capacity_pool`, `capacity_reading` and `capacity_notice`. The output rows are `platform_capacity_basis(period, free, used)` and `tenant_capacity_summary(tenant, period, free, used, own)`. Tenant roles may read their own readings and both public notices.

In the second state only the late declaration is withdrawn. An extra test rule supplies the same late summary from the private platform basis. Both summaries must still be provable in a real complete publication. The early tenant witness remains readable. The platform's publication policy requires both summaries addressed to that tenant. If one would require a private premise, it refuses the entire new tenant manifest before it becomes visible, preserving the previous manifest. Ordinary filtering of other tenants' unrelated conclusions remains possible.

The test follows the real publisher's manifests, snapshots, witnesses and source positions. It checks each positive edge against readable rows, absences and aggregates against full relations, aggregate groups against their contributing rows, and calculation premises against their snapshots. It also looks for the other tenant's identifier across the published role objects. The small independent two-hop graph demonstrates that a private leaf or a redacted placeholder invalidates an otherwise readable result.
