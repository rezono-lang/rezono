# Licence scope for reference platform components

Decision maker: Rezono project owner.

Rezono applies its dependency licence rules to its releases, its own images and its runtime dependencies. Its oracles and tools follow the allowance for unshipped tools when they are used only for development or verification.

The reference platform deploys products as separate, unchanged images published by their suppliers. Deploying one of these images keeps the product outside Rezono's dependencies. Extending the release allowlist to these images would make a deployment choice subject to a rule about what Rezono ships.

Record each product's licence and its source as product knowledge. A rule presents the licence for each deployed component as information for the reader, outside the set of policy findings.
