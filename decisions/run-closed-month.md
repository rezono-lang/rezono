# Reproduce a closed billing month from the retained edition

The close decision binds the month, UTC cutoff and digest of the cost publication. Project a std-decision decision with these values. Retain the exact edition bytes in the publisher's immutable store and check their SHA-256 on every replay. Refuse an unavailable or mismatched digest. A newer edition has a new digest and can be used by another decision. Replaying an earlier decision always resolves its own digest.

In `run/closed_month.py`, expose `retain(store, bytes)` and `report(store, decision, observations)` for offline replay. The fixture's small price publication gives rational EUR per billed line. Each run observation provides its item, plan identity, received instant, and the service interval or setup instant. Half-open UTC month boundaries split a period in proportion to elapsed time. Assign each setup charge once to the UTC month of occurrence. Only arrivals strictly before the close can contribute. Return exact rational totals and line allocations. The late finding records the observation and the specific close decision. A later arrival may change these findings, while leaving the closed amounts intact. Using the latest edition, the service time as arrival time, or a local calendar would change the fixture's result.
