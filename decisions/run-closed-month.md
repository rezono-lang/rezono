# Reproduce a closed billing month from the retained edition

The close decision binds the month, UTC cutoff and digest of the cost publication. Project a std-decision decision with these values. Retain the exact edition bytes in the publisher's immutable store and check their SHA-256 on every replay. Refuse an unavailable or mismatched digest. A newer edition has a new digest and can be used by another decision. Replaying an earlier decision always resolves its own digest.

In `run/closed_month.py`, expose `retain(store, bytes)` and `report(store, decision, observations)` for offline replay. The fixture's small price publication gives rational EUR per billed line. Each run observation provides its item, plan identity, received instant, and the service interval or setup instant. Half-open UTC month boundaries split a period in proportion to elapsed time. Assign each setup charge once to the UTC month of occurrence. Only arrivals strictly before the close can contribute. Return exact rational totals and line allocations. The late finding records the observation and the specific close decision. A later arrival may change these findings, while leaving the closed amounts intact. Using the latest edition, the service time as arrival time, or a local calendar would change the fixture's result.

## Coverage of the intake

A close publishes amounts only for an intake proved complete at its cutoff. The close decision pins `completeness_receipts`: archived references to the candidate receipts of the billing intake. The trusted intake context comes from the protected caller as `report(store, decision, observations, authorities=())`, never from a candidate. Admission is the shared check of [scoped completeness](scoped-completeness.md) (`rezono_checker.completeness.admit`); the close adds no receipt check of its own.

The close then requires exactly one admitted receipt whose trusted scope binds `closed_at` to this cutoff, and requires the timely observations to equal the certified rows by identity, plan hash and content. Only then does the report publish `lines` and `total_eur`, with `coverage` holding the `verified_receipt` row and an aggregate witness of the total: the number of contributing rows and the SHA-256 of their canonical rows in id order. Otherwise `lines` and `total_eur` are null and `coverage.unknown` lists why: `receipt_missing`, the admission code of a rejected candidate, `receipt_not_at_close`, `receipt_ambiguous` or `receipt_replay_mismatch` with the missing, unexpected or changed ids. Late findings are reported in both cases. A certified empty intake closes at zero; an empty or partial delivery without an admitted receipt does not close. Validation of each observation, such as its plan digest, still fails before admission.

The allocation stays in Python with exact rational splits. Rules over the certified contents, with `complete` from `std-completeness` as a positive premise, can replace it once that pack exists, without changing the report.
