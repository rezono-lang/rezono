# Sweep the price at which the selected offer changes

Extend the run's offer-selection pack with a three-point exploration of the period price for bid amber. Use the ordinary compiler sweep declaration, delta writer and publisher epoch evaluator. All candidates meet the same capacity, boot, disk, currency and virtualization requirements. Both binding quotes and reject-above orders can enforce the entire charge for two billing periods including setup. The unprotected estimate cannot win.

The independently chosen terms in EUR cents are two periods at amber's variable price plus 300 setup, or two at blue's fixed 600 plus 100 setup. Amber wins the stable tie rank. The changeover is exactly 500 cents per period. Sweep 499, 500 and 501, preserving all other observations. A third candidate, clear, charges one cent per period and has zero setup, but initially supplies only an estimate. Changing that one guarantee into a binding total must make it win. Render `selected(demand, offer)` and `payable_minor(demand, offer, amount)` from the actual run pack. Its three stable offer IDs are amber, blue and clear. Binding terms use `guarantee_mode` on the offer. A separate sorting function would not demonstrate that the model and the published exploration agree.
