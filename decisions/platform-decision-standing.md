# An effective placement may also require review

Each zone matrix, reviewer judgement, product stance, capacity statement and monthly cost binds a real std-decision Decision to its platform content. Placements and exclusions have their own decisions. Evaluate them at written reference dates. Import the pinned theory and consume in_force_on, replaced and ended. The original recorded state alone cannot establish standing.

On April 8 an outbound judgement is superseded. Two active placements rest on separate grounds. One ground is explicitly replaced that day while the other simply expires. Both placements keep contributing their conclusions. Only the replacement requires review, with the original ground, its successor and the dependent decision cited. Removing the supersedes edge makes that review disappear. An independently effective exclusion contradicts the first placement on April 8, then expires on April 10. The placement survives both dates, and the conflict proves both decision standings.

Run `python -m pytest -q examples/platform/proof_cases/test_decision_standing.py`. The standard-theory smoke case already uses real compiled rows. Add the import and typed domain bindings in `examples/platform/pack.yaml`, `schema` and `rules`.
