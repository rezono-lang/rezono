# Database choice and service selectors in admission

Changing the platform's database provider for the identity service replaces the old same-zone database grant with the new one. The Git service's independent choice keeps its own grant. A checked publication for each edition supplies its own digest, namespaced parameter rows and corresponding CEL policy binding. A requested grant must follow the choice in that edition in both directions.

The platform generator identifies a service with exactly one service label. Kubernetes combines selector requirements by conjunction. An explicitly empty `matchExpressions` list adds zero requirements, so it selects exactly the same service as omitting the list. The managed preflight and the released CEL policy must agree on these two representations at the local and peer endpoints. Nonempty expressions remain outside the exact-service mapping. This case exercises a fixed TCP service, independently of extending the publication to other transports.
